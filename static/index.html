HOW IT WORKS

From upload to verdict in five steps

1UploadJPEG or PNG, one or twelve
2Read the fileC2PA, XMP, EXIF, PNG text
3Two classifiersV1 paper model, V2 extended
4EvidenceELA, spectrum, noise, stress

WHAT IT DOES

Not one score. A whole investigation.

◆

Provenance reader

Reads C2PA Content Credentials, XMP digital-source-type and the generation parameters that Stable Diffusion WebUI, ComfyUI and NovelAI write into PNG files, plus the hints ChatGPT and Firefly leave behind.

⬢

Twin classifiers

The paper’s ADM model (V1) and an extended model (V2) trained on 60+ generators.

▦

Error Level Analysis

Where does JPEG re-compression disagree with itself?

∿

Spectrum fingerprint

Radial frequency profile and 2D spectrum, ready to inspect.

⁘

Noise residual

What is left when the image is median-filtered away.

⚡

Stress test

Re-runs both classifiers after JPEG q75 and q50, resize, blur and crop. A verdict that flips under mild damage is a fragile verdict, and you will see it.
